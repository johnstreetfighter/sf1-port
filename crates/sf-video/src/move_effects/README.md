# Move effect sources

SpritePilot released the source art for these effects under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `landing-dust-16px_strip7.png` is the original 16 px strip. [Effects Pack](https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/effects)
- `parry-flash-16px_strip7.png` is the original 16 px strip. [Combat Pack](https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/combat)
- `ken-fire-16px_strip8.png` adapts the Effects Pack's 16 px fire strip. The flame points down and back from the rising fist, with a warmer red, orange, and yellow palette. [Fire source](https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/effects#fire)
- `ken-burn-16px_strip7.png` layers three clipped flames from the same strip in each of its three ignition frames. Four new ember frames follow. [Fire source](https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/effects#fire)

The Denjin, Tiger Cannon, and Zan'ei sheets are original art for the preview. Each uses fully opaque color ramps and transparent pixels on the native game grid.

- `denjin-hands.png`, `denjin-projectile.png`, and `denjin-impact.png` use irregular blue-white arcs and a few gold sparks to link the charge, travel, and contact.
- `tiger-cannon.png` extends the native projectile with a broad gold nose and a striped wake. `tiger-impact.png` spreads into a low gold ring.
- `zanei-impact.png` gives Gen's strike a thin pale slash with a violet edge and a short dissipating tail.
- `hit-light.png`, `hit-medium.png`, and `hit-heavy.png` are original contact sparks. Their silhouettes and duration grow with hit strength.
- The original Drive Impact strips use red-orange paint on startup, swing, and clean contact, plus a cool guard arc. [Capcom's SF6 paint and graffiti reference](https://news.capcomusa.com/2022/06/02/street-fighter-6-redefines-the-genre-in-2023/)
- `slam-dust.png` is original art for hard ground contacts. Its low dust clouds spread to either side of the fighter on landing.
