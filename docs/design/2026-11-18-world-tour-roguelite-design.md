# Roguelite World Tour, hybrid builds, active tag and prop minigames: design

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Status:** Approved 2026-11-18; Spec #240 and tickets `WT1`–`WT16` (`#241`–`#256`) published (with `CONTEXT.md` + `ADR-0028` updated). Nothing here is built.
- **Size:** Project (replacing the static 18-node World Tour with a seeded roguelite run, between-round 3-card drafts, escalating loss burns, meta-progression starting unlocks, slot-shared `TourBuild` modifiers/statuses/cross-era move grafting/CPU specialists, 3rd-slot active tag swaps, and 2,048-column side-scrolling prop minigames).
- **Marking:** `"(user)"` is the maintainer's decision in brainstorming; `"(user accepted)"` is a proposal the maintainer confirmed in playback; `"My call"` is the designer's choice, which the maintainer has not been asked about one by one.

## Intent and the Why

Today's World Tour (`crates/sf-engine/src/world_tour/native.rs`, `crates/sf-engine/src/front/world_tour.rs`, `crates/sf-engine/src/front/session/world_tour.rs`, `crates/sf-engine/src/era_fight/world_tour.rs`, `ADR-0016`) is a fixed 18-node checklist across the five 1987 countries backed by one shared profile, three 0–5 stats, five perks, no story, and XP paid only on a node's first clear. Because `docs/roadmap.md` keeps every fighter, era style, costume, and palette unlocked from the start across Arcade, Local, Training, and Online, World Tour cannot rely on gating the core roster elsewhere; once a player finishes those 18 fights in under an hour, there is little reason to return.

This revamp turns World Tour into a **single-player roguelite run** (`(user)`): a dedicated home for creative fight variations, engine-stretching builds, silliness, *Street Fighter II: Rainbow Edition* / *Koryu*-style chaos, and intense retro-homage minigames that would not belong in balanced multiplayer (`(user)`). Every run begins barebones and lets the player assemble an outrageous hybrid fighter round by round, while clearing runs and milestones unlocks new starting options for future runs (`(user)`).

This design supersedes the World Tour portion of `ADR-0016` while leaving `ADR-0016`'s `2v1` Dramatic Battle and 12-fighter ROM-composite Arcade endings untouched. It also updates how open Bonus Challenger and Fair CPU World Tour tickets (`#33`, `#51`, `#64`, `#215`) fit in: instead of appending static nodes `19..33` to a fixed graph, released Bonus Challengers and the stronger Fair CPU feed directly into World Tour's run node pools, specialist templates, partner bench, and meta-unlock tracks. It will require a new ADR and `CONTEXT.md` glossary updates in the next workflow phase (`grill-with-docs`).

## Goals

1. **From vanilla `SF1` to wild late-run hybrids (`(user)`):** A fresh World Tour save starts in vanilla `SF1` with no era styles available inside the mode (`(user)`). Over a single run, the player unlocks and stacks Era Styles, cross-era grafted moves from that fighter's other eras (for example, an `SFZ3` Ryu with Ice-infused attacks and `SF6` command normals), elemental effects, *Rainbow Edition* mutators, and a tag partner (`(user)`).
2. **Per-round and per-match 3-card upgrade drafts (`(user)`):** Winning each round immediately presents three upgrade cards before the next round begins; winning the match locks in that match's round picks, grants one bonus 3-card draft (three total upgrades per won match), and restores the player to full health (`(user)`).
3. **Upgrades as your lifeline (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`):** Losing a match discards any unbanked round picks from that match and costs banked upgrades (`(user)`): continuing costs `1`, `2`, `3`… banked upgrades of the player's choice to step back onto the map, or triggers **Run Over** when the player cannot pay (`(user accepted)`).
4. **Build-specialized opponents (`(user)`):** Runs feature CPU opponents constructed around extreme build archetypes—such as all-fire specialists, status specialists who slow the player down, and speed specialists whose animations run up to 4× normal speed (`(user)`).
5. **Retro-homage minigames with extensible props (`(user)`):** Runs mix in intense minigames—highlighted by a left-to-right *Super Mario Bros.*-style scrolling course with breakable and dodgeable props—launching immediately with 1987 ROM props and expanding through a data-driven prop registry as PixelLab credits renew (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`).
6. **Between-run starting unlocks (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`):** Finishing a run (or hitting milestone clears before a Run Over) unlocks new starting choices—fighters, era styles, and starting relics—that were not available at the start of prior runs (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`), while leaving Arcade, Local, Dramatic Battle, Training, Online, and Matchmaking completely unlocked from day one (`(user)`).

## Non-goals

- Gating any fighter, era style, costume, stage, or palette outside World Tour (`(user)`).
- Grafting moves from a *different* fighter whose sprite poses the active fighter does not have (`(user accepted)` — grafted command normals and specials stay within the active fighter's own built eras).
- Grafting metered or era-system donor moves (`EX`/`OD` specials, `Drive` Impact/Parry/Rush/Reversal, `Focus` Attack, `V-Skill`/`V-Trigger`, `Super Arts`), which depend on era gauges that `SF1`, `SF1+`, and `V-ISM` lack (`(user)`).
- Blocking launch on external PixelLab prop art generation (`(user accepted)` — launch with ROM props first, then grow the prop library incrementally).
- Auto-saving `.sf1replay` recordings for World Tour bouts or minigames (`*My call:*` keeps today's `leave_world_tour_fight` call to `finish_replay_recording(false)`, avoiding `.sf1replay` format churn for mid-match draft pauses and uncapped scrolling courses).
- Online or local 2-player co-op World Tour runs.

## Anti-goals

- **Zero leakage into `Faithful 1987` or competitive modes:** No World Tour build modifier, status effect, `TourConstruct`, animation-speed multiplier, uncapped camera scroll, or cross-era grafted move ever fires outside World Tour fights (`FightKind::WorldTour` and World Tour's Dramatic/Tag encounters). Newly authored canonical `SF6` command normals for `Ryu`, `Ken`, and `Retsu` belong natively to `EraFamily::Sf6` across overhaul modes and never touch `Faithful 1987` (`(user)`). All 137 MAME reference scenarios remain bit-for-bit identical.
- **No parallel combat engine:** Do not bring back standalone 2D combat structs outside `Fight` (`ADR-0004`, `ADR-0016`). Every bout, tag swap, and side-scrolling prop minigame runs on the translated 1987 machine.
- **No 25-loss slogs or random build destruction:** Escalating burn costs prevent dragging out a doomed late run across dozens of losses, and the player always chooses which banked upgrade(s) to burn.

## Constraints

- **3-slot fighter and object-RAM layout (`crates/sf-engine/src/fighter.rs`, `tasks.rs`, `ADR-0016`):** Keep the engine's 3-slot limit (`(user)`) with no 4th fighter slot: Slot `0` is `P1_STRUCT` (`0xff86b0`, object RAM `0x400..0x9ff`), Slot `1` is `CPU_STRUCT` (`0xffc664`, object RAM `0xa00..0xfff`), Slot `2` is `P2_STRUCT` (`0xff86da`, object RAM `0x1000..0x15ff`), and `0xffe100..0xffe3ff` provides twelve `0x40`-byte projectile/effect object slots.
- **Art-pack requirement for non-shoto fighters (`crates/sf-engine/src/front/session/world_tour.rs`):** Only Ryu and Ken have native 1987 ROM player move tables; the ten 1987 bosses require the local `SF1+` art pack (`player_tables()`) to play overhaul kits, `TourBuild` moves, or tag/playable roles. `*My call:*` Entering World Tour checks that all 12 tournament fighters are present in `player_tables()` and surfaces the existing `NEEDS THE SF1+ ART PACK` notice if missing. Bonus challengers enter World Tour pools automatically whenever their roster wave is released and `config.roster.bonus_challengers` is enabled.
- **2,048-column `BackgroundBank` pages (`crates/sf-video/src/background_bank.rs`, `ADR-0009`):** Each extended background map page holds `PAGE_COLS = 2048` columns (`32,768` pixels across `16` rows) on both `Bg` (`gfx1`) and `Fg` (`gfx2`) layers. Standard fights clamp `CAMERA` (`0xffc680`) to `0..=0x180`; uncapping `CAMERA` up to a course's pixel width under a World Tour minigame rule enables multi-screen left-to-right scrolling on the existing video renderer.
- **Indexed move-effect strips (`crates/sf-video/src/move_effects.rs`, `signature.rs`):** Embedded 16-color indexed strips (`KenFire`, `KenBurn`, `DenjinHands`, `DenjinProjectile`, `DenjinImpact`, `Satsui`, `Zanei`, `LandingDust`, `Parry`) carry explicit `palette` arrays (`[u32; 16]`) on `IndexedSprite`, allowing Ice/Frost, Fire, Shock, and Venom overlays immediately via palette swaps without waiting on new art.
- **Six-button cabinet input, scheme compatibility, and pad-only UI (`crates/sf-engine/src/control.rs`, `front/rebind.rs`, `#129`):** Every World Tour screen, draft modal, and burn picker must navigate purely via `MenuInput` on a controller/Steam Deck. No new keybinds are added (`bindings.toml` and `RebindScreen` stay untouched) (`(user)`). Because `ControlScheme::Modern` lacks `HK` (`[light, medium, heavy, special, assist, macro_button]`) and `ControlScheme::Pneumatic` fires light attacks on release and heavy on press (and 2-button setups omit `HK`), Active Tag and Tag Cancel are detected on **raw pre-translation inputs** before `translate_player_controls` runs (`(user)`): **`LP+HK`** under Classic, **`Special+Macro`** under Modern, and **`Down, Down + Punch+Kick`** under Pneumatic, suppressing the normal/macro output on the trigger frame.

## Approaches

1. **Continuous `Fight` overlay + runtime `TourBuild` dispatch + `Fight`-driven minigames — Chosen (`(user)`):**
   - Matches run inside a single continuous `Fight` (`crates/sf-engine/src/front/fight.rs`). When `P1` wins a round, `GameSession` pauses immediately after the round-result tally behind a 3-card draft overlay, writes the chosen upgrade into `TourBuild` inside `CoreToggleState`, and resumes so Round 2 starts natively with gauges and round markers intact.
   - All three fighter slots (`0..3`) carry a `TourBuild` in `CoreToggleState`. Grafted command normals and specials redirect at input-recognition time to the donor era's existing `DataBank` move slot/stream for that fighter (alongside newly authored signature `SF6` command normals) rather than generating combinatorial move tables. Active Tag uses slot `2` (`P2_STRUCT` / `0x1000..0x15ff`) for a leap-in entry strike when slot `2` is free and swaps in-place when slot `2` is occupied (`1v2` Ambushes / `2v1` Finales). Side-scrolling homage minigames run inside `Fight` over a `2,048`-column `BackgroundBank` page with uncapped `CAMERA` and hittable/hazardous prop objects.
   - *Trade-off:* Keeps native round flow, real fighter physics and upgrades in minigames, and zero `DataBank` combinatorial bloat, at the cost of disciplined `world_tour_rules()` gating in `fighter.rs` and clean donor-move state resets when grafted attacks return to neutral.
2. **Per-round `Fight` reboots + synthesized `DataBank` tables + standalone `MinigameScreen` — Rejected:**
   - Reboots a fresh single-round `Fight` after each round draft (extending today's `SurvivalGauntlet` wave loop in `front/session/world_tour.rs`), synthesizes a custom 64-slot move table in scratch `DataBank` memory for each hybrid kit, and runs scrolling minigames on a separate `Screen` outside `FightMachine`. Rejected because rebooting `Fight` mid-match breaks native round flow unless win icons and era meters are manually patched back into RAM, complicates mid-round partner swaps, and forces minigames to duplicate simplified fighter physics instead of letting your real upgraded build interact with props.
3. **Dual-live structs (`P1_STRUCT` + `P2_STRUCT`) for Active Tag — Rejected:**
   - Uses Approach 1's continuous `Fight` draft overlay, runtime `TourBuild` dispatch, and `Fight`-driven minigames, except lead and partner stay permanently resident in `P1_STRUCT` and `P2_STRUCT` all round and Tag routes `P1`'s pad inputs between them while parking the inactive partner off-screen. Rejected because although rapid tag-cancels avoid migrating charge buffers, dozens of checks across `tasks.rs`, `era_fight/world_tour.rs`, HUD bars, and KO handling assume the human player is always `P1_STRUCT`, widening engine risk unnecessarily.

### Card & Construct Engine

1. **Pre-baked `TourBuild` event hooks + dedicated `TourConstruct` array alongside native `0xffe100` projectiles — Chosen (`(user)`):**
   - Each slot's `TourBuild` stores its `5` Channel infusions, `8` Ailment counters, `3` Grafted + Omega slots, Bootleg/Cursed flags, and a compiled modifier summary recomputed whenever cards change, so `fighter.rs` only invokes compact event hooks (`on_move_start`, `on_contact`, `on_guard`, `on_taunt`, `on_whiff`, `on_tick`) rather than branching across 80+ individual card IDs.
   - Standard extra projectiles (*Rainbow* volleys, *Red Wave* normal shockwaves, *Xiang Long* rising-uppercut fireballs) use the 12 native `0xffe100..0xffe3ff` object-RAM slots so projectile clashes and *Soul Power* reflection work natively, while persistent/timed constructs (**Orbiting Soul Orbs**, **Tengu Stones**, **Psycho Mines**, **Agni Fire Carpets**, and **12-frame Spirit Echoes**) live in a fixed-size `Copy` `TourConstruct` array inside `CoreToggleState` (snapshotted in `FightSnapshot` and composited via `OverhaulScene`) so constructs never starve the 12 native hit-spark/projectile slots.
   - *Trade-off:* Prevents orbiting orbs, mines, and carpets from starving hit sparks and fireball storms while keeping `fighter.rs` uncluttered and deterministic; adds one small World Tour construct collision and rendering loop alongside native projectiles.
2. **Cram all Orbs, Stones, Mines, and Carpets into the 12 native `0xffe100` slots via `DataBank` routines — Rejected:**
   - Reuses one collision loop, but sharing 12 slots across both fighters' hit sparks, smoke puffs, multi-fireballs, orbiting orbs, and mines will saturate constantly and silently drop spawns while spreading binary stream authoring across every card ticket.
3. **External TOML/bytecode card-scripting VM in `sf-engine` — Rejected:**
   - Flexible for external modding, but heavy to keep zero-allocation `Copy` inside `FightSnapshot`, slower across `cargo suite`, and harder to debug on frame-exact rules like *Spirit Echo* pose replays or *Omega* cancel windows than typed Rust tables.

## What was decided

### 1. Fresh start and between-run meta-progression

- **Barebones initial state (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`):** A brand-new World Tour profile starts with **Ryu** and **Ken** unlocked in vanilla **`SF1`** (`EraFamily::Sf1987`), zero Era Styles unlocked as starting options, and no starting relics (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`).
- **Run-1 mid-run pool vs. between-run Starting unlocks (`(user)`):** Flagship **Era Style Shift** cards (`SF1+`, `SF2 ST`, `SFZ3`, `SF3 3S`, `USF4`, `SF5 CE`, `SF6`, and built `SF KESSEN`) and **Partner Contract** cards for all built tournament fighters (plus released Bonus Challengers when `config.roster.bonus_challengers` is enabled) are already in the mid-run draft pool from **Run 1**, so every run can climb out of `SF1` and recruit any built partner mid-run (`(user)`). Inside World Tour, built what-if styles are available regardless of the multiplayer `what_if_styles` toggle so every fighter has full hybrid variety (`*My call:*`).
- **Pre-run setup and three unlock tracks (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`):** Ending a run unlocks new options on a pre-run **Run Setup** screen—and gates alt Era revisions/loadouts and Cursed Cards—across three tracks (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`):
  1. **Starting Fighters:** Unlocks the ten 1987 tournament bosses (plus released Bonus Challengers when `config.roster.bonus_challengers` is enabled) for Run Setup. Because only Ryu and Ken have native `SF1` (`Arcade1987`) tables, recruited bosses and bonus challengers default to `SF1+` as their starting base style (`(user accepted)`).
  2. **Starting Era Styles:** Unlocks `SF1+`, `SF2`, `SFZ`, `SF3`, `SF4`, `SF5`, `SF6`, and `SF KESSEN` (where built for the chosen fighter) as starting styles on Run Setup (`(user)`), and unlocks that family's **non-flagship Era revisions and loadouts** (`Old ST`, `X-ISM` / `V-ISM`, `SA2` / `SA3`, `Ultra II`, `VT2`, etc.) in both Run Setup and mid-run drafts (`(user)`).
  3. **Starting Relics:** Unlocks one Fight-1 keepsake slot for the run (such as starting with an Elemental Core, a pre-signed Partner contract, a *Rainbow* bootleg chip, or an unlocked Cursed Relic).
- **How unlocks are earned (`*My call:*` + `(user)`):** First-time clears of a Country Boss (`Japan`, `USA`, `China`, `England`) in a run bank `1` meta-unlock pick for the end of that run, and clearing the `Thailand` Grand Finale awards `2` meta-unlock picks (with repeat clears still granting `1` pick if locked items remain). At **Run Clear** or **Run Over**, any earned unlock picks present a 3-card choice of locked items across the three tracks so every meaningful run expands future runs' starting possibilities. **Cursed Cards** sit in the rarest unlock and draft tier (`(user)`): Cursed Cards enter the **Starting unlock** pool only after the profile has cleared `Thailand` at least once (`*My call:*`), remaining locked on early profiles to prevent Run-1 self-sabotage while giving veterans ultra-high-risk chase unlocks usable both as rare late-run drops and as Cursed Starting Relics (`(user)`).
- **Pure-run challenge switch (`*My call:*`):** Run Setup includes a `PURE RUN: OFF / ON` toggle (`OFF` by default) for purists who want to play a seeded run's crazy enemy archetypes and minigames without mid-run upgrade cards.

### 2. Run map, pacing, round/match drafts, and loss burns

- **World travel map backdrop (`crates/sf-engine/src/front/world_tour.rs`, `ADR-0016`):** Keep the 1987 `0x3f812` world travel map (`fg_scroll = 0x1980`, palette `0x1a268`) as the run map backdrop, drawing connected route nodes, clear stars, and specialist/minigame badges over the map.
- **Seeded 5-country branching run (`*My call:*`):** Starting a run generates a seeded branching tour across `Japan -> USA / China -> England -> Thailand` (`10–12` stops total: `2–3` nodes per country mixing Specialist Bouts, *Rainbow* Twist / `1v2` Ambush Bouts, at least `1` Homage Minigame stop per region, and a Country Finale Boss; ~20–30 minutes per run). Every map node previews the opponent fighter(s), specialist archetype badge, or minigame title.
- **Round and match 3-card drafts (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`):**
  - Winning **any round** of a bout immediately opens a 3-card upgrade draft overlay inside `GameSession` before the next round starts; the chosen card applies to `P1`'s `TourBuild` right away for the following round (`(user)`).
  - Winning **the match** locks those round picks into the run's banked upgrade list, opens one bonus 3-card draft (yielding **three total upgrades** per won 2-of-3 match), and restores `P1` to full `960` Sub-HP before returning to the map (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`).
  - Defeating a build-specialized opponent guarantees at least one card matching their Lore School or Bootleg theme in the match-win draft (`(user accepted)`).
  - **4-School Run Pool (`(user accepted)`):** To help builds reach Rank 3 and Duo Capstones instead of diluting across all eight schools, once a run's `TourBuild` holds cards from `4` distinct **Lore Schools** (`*My call:*` exact cap = `4`, counting any Starting Relic's school), subsequent school drafts draw only from those `4` active schools (plus universal Bootleg Chips, Grafted/Omega cards, Partner cards, and unlocked Cursed Cards).
- **Escalating loss burns, pause rules, and Run Over (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`):**
  - Losing a match discards any unbanked round picks from that match and increments the run's defeat counter `L` (`1` on first loss, `2` on second loss, `3` on third, and so on) (`(user accepted)`).
  - **Soulbound Cursed Cards (`(user accepted)`):** Once drafted (or equipped as a Cursed Starting Relic), a **Cursed Card** cannot be burned in a **Loss burn**—only clean (non-cursed) banked cards can pay the defeat cost `L`.
  - If the player holds at least `L` burnable (non-cursed) banked upgrades, a **Burn `L` Upgrades** modal lets the player choose exactly which `L` banked card(s) to sacrifice, strips their effects from `TourBuild`, and steps the player back onto the map (allowing a rematch or taking an alternate branch/minigame) (`(user accepted)`).
  - If the player has fewer than `L` burnable banked upgrades (including losing Fight 1 with `0` burnable cards), the run ends in **Run Over** (`(user accepted)`), still awarding any meta-unlock picks earned from country milestones already cleared in that run (`(user accepted)`).
  - World Tour combat bouts use a dedicated pause menu (`PauseKind::WorldTour`) that removes `RESTART ROUND` and replaces `QUIT TO MENU` with **`FORFEIT MATCH`** (`(user)`). Choosing `FORFEIT MATCH` mid-bout—or force-closing the game mid-bout—counts as a match defeat so players cannot dodge Loss Burns (`(user)`). Quitting from the world map saves cleanly with no penalty (`(user)`).
- **Card tiers (`*My call:*`):** To keep ~25–30 upgrades across a full run clean and exciting without cluttering inputs, cards divide into **Major Cards** (base Era Style shifts, Grafted Moves, *Omega* Graft Masteries, Partner Contracts, School Channel infusions, *Eclipse* Keystones, Duo Capstones, Cursed Cards) and **Rank / Synergy Cards** (leveling owned channel infusions, mutators, grafted slots, or partners from Rank 1 to Rank 3, + projectile count, + status potency, or meter/vitality bonuses). Early rounds weight Era Style shifts and first channel/move unlocks heavily so players rapidly escape `SF1`'s barebones start.

### 3. Shared `TourBuild` engine (player, CPU specialists, and partners)

- **Unified `[TourBuild; 3]` state (`(user accepted)`):** Today's `era_fight/world_tour.rs` hard-codes World Tour bonuses to `P1_STRUCT`. Instead, `CoreToggleState` stores `TourBuild` (including `5` Channel infusions, `8` Ailment counters, `3` Grafted + Omega slots, Bootleg/Cursed flags, and pre-baked event hooks) plus the fixed-size `TourConstruct` array for all three fighter slots (`0 = P1_STRUCT`, `1 = CPU_STRUCT`, `2 = Partner / 2nd CPU`) inside `WorldTourRules`, so every School effect, ailment, construct, Bootleg chip, grafted move, and Cursed card runs uniformly for the player, enemy **Specialists**, and **Tag partners** (`(user accepted)`).
- **Base Era Style unlocks mid-run (`(user)`):** Flagship **Era Style Shift** cards (`SF1+`, `SF2 ST`, `SFZ3`, `SF3 3S`, `USF4`, `SF5 CE`, `SF6`, and built `SF KESSEN`) are draftable from Run 1, while non-flagship revisions/loadouts join the draft pool once their Era Family is unlocked (`(user)`). Drafting an Era Style Shift card switches the fighter's active `StylePick` (for example from `SF1` into `SFZ3` or `SF6`), initializing that era's gauge and base move list on the next round.
- **A. Eight Canon *Street Fighter* Lore Schools (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`):**
  1. **Satsui no Hado (Dark Hado):** Crimson/black `Satsui` aura; **Wither** ailment (stacks amplify incoming hit and chip damage, allowing specials to chip-KO at max stacks); Ashura phase-step dash invulnerability, multi-hit dark projectiles, and HP-sacrifice berserker buffs.
  2. **Kyoi no Hado / Mu no Ken (Power of Nothingness):** White/cyan `Denjin` aura; **Shock** ailment (adds bonus hitstun, blockstun, and dizzy/stun build-up, discharging a Denjin jolt at max stacks); Kongoshin counter-parry windows, neutral-stance debuff cleansing, and calm/high-HP bonuses.
  3. **Psycho Power:** Violet flame aura; **Fear** on guarded hits (drains defender's Era gauge and accelerates guard pushback/crush); **Psycho Mines** (`TourConstruct` charges planted on contact or stage that detonate after a short delay for juggle pop-ups); telekinetic pull on hits and projectiles.
  4. **Soul Power:** Emerald/gold astral aura; orbiting **Soul Orbs** (`TourConstruct` satellites that intercept enemy projectiles or launch with heavy attacks); projectile reflection on timely guard/parry (`0xffe100` owner/velocity reversal); Era-gauge siphon; and **Tarot Foresight** (bonus draft rerolls).
  5. **Spirit Power (Thunderfoot Wind & 1987 Departed Masters):** Jade wind / spirit aura honoring Thunderfoot wind spirits and departed 1987 masters (Gou Hibiki, Goutetsu); **Wind stocks** built by advancing or brief neutral focus that armor and accelerate the next dash, throw, or special; **Spirit Echo** (`TourConstruct` afterimage replaying a special strike `12` frames later at reduced damage).
  6. **Agni Yoga & Illuminati Cryo:** Agni (**Burn** ticking non-lethal Sub-HP damage floored at `1` Sub-HP + lingering **Fire Carpet** `TourConstruct`s, Yoga float/teleport options) and Cryo (**Chill** slowing walk/dash speed and stretching frame holds `0x33` by `1.5×–2×`, triggering a brief freeze stun at max stacks). Drafting both unlocks Gill's **Thermal Shock** (Fire on Chilled or Ice on Burning triggers an immediate burst) and once-per-run **Resurrection** (`*My call:*` revives at `30%` Sub-HP once per run on a round-losing KO).
  7. **Senjutsu & Assassin Arts:** Amber stone / toxic magenta aura (Oro, Gen, Geki); orbiting **Tengu Stones** (`TourConstruct` rocks hurled alongside attacks), smoke-vanish dodges, **Venom** (boosted chip damage + special-move Sub-HP leech), and Gen's **Death-Touch (*Shi*)** mark (accumulates up to `5` marks on normals and ruptures for burst damage on the `5`th mark or a landed special).
  8. **Saikyo & Street Grit:** Hot-pink / gold sparkle aura (Dan, Metro City, Hakan); **Swagger** (completing Taunt builds meter and charges `2.5×` damage on the next landed hit) and **Oil Drench** (amplifies ground slide and doubles the next **Burn** or **Shock** hit/ailment, consuming Oil).
- **B. Lore Rivalries, Duo Capstones, & *Eclipse* Fusions (`(user accepted)`):**
  - Opposing pairs (**Satsui no Hado** ↔ **Kyoi no Hado**, **Psycho Power** ↔ **Soul Power**) lock each other out of the run's draft pool once either side is picked—unless the player drafts a rare ***Eclipse* Keystone** (*Twilight Hado* or *Shared Soul*), which fuses both opposites (`(user accepted)`).
  - Compatible school pairs unlock **Duo Capstones** once both schools are held (such as *Satsui + Psycho* = **Violent Awakening**, *Kyoi + Spirit* = **Tempest Denjin**, *Soul + Senjutsu* = **Astral Tengu Storm**, and *Agni + Cryo* = **Thermal Shock / Resurrection**) (`(user accepted)`).
- **C. Five Kit Channels (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`):**
  - Core Lore School infusions bind to **5 Kit Channels**: `Normals`, `Specials` (covering base specials and all **Grafted moves**), `Projectiles`, `Movement`, and `Defense/Utility`, allowing deliberate cross-school combos (e.g., *Saikyo Oil* on `Normals` feeding *Kyoi Shock* on `Specials`).
  - Each channel holds one School infusion (`Rank 1–3`), prompting a Channel Swap modal if a new school card is drafted onto an occupied channel (`*My call:*`). On **Active Tag**, Lead and **Tag partner** share the run's `5` Channel infusions, Bootleg Chips, Duo Capstones, and Cursed Cards while keeping separate Era Gauges and `3` Grafted + Omega slots (`*My call:*`).
- **D. Cross-era Move Grafting & *Omega* Graft Mastery (`WT11`, `(user)` + `(user accepted)`):**
  - Drafting a **Grafted Move** card attaches a meterless attack special or direct-attack command normal from another built era of the **same fighter** (e.g., `SFZ3` Ryu grafting `SF6` Ryu's command normals or Hashogeki) (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`).

### 4. Active Tag Partner within the 3-fighter-slot engine

- **Recruiting and sharing (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`):** Partner Contract cards recruit a second tournament fighter (with their own `StylePick` and optional grafted moves) onto `P1`'s bench, displayed as a compact reserve portrait/icon beneath `P1`'s life bar. Both partners share `P1`'s `960` Sub-HP bar and the run's `5` Channel infusions, Bootleg Chips, Duo Capstones, and Cursed Cards, while maintaining separate Era Gauges and `3` Grafted + Omega slots (`(user accepted)`).
- **Scheme-specific pre-translation Tag inputs (`(user)`):** Active Tag and Tag Cancel are read from raw pre-translation player inputs with no new keybinds added to `bindings.toml` or `RebindScreen`, suppressing normal/macro attack output on trigger:
  - `ControlScheme::Classic`: **`LP+HK`** (`buttons[0] && buttons[5]`).
  - `ControlScheme::Modern`: **`Special+Macro`** (`raw[3] && raw[5]`).
  - `ControlScheme::Pneumatic`: **`Down, Down + Punch+Kick`** (`Down, Neutral, Down` followed by simultaneous punch-pad and kick-pad press).
- **3-slot execution across encounter types (`(user)` + `(user accepted)`):**
  - **Standard `1v1` bouts & side-scrolling minigames:** Slot `2` (`P2_STRUCT`, object RAM `0x1000..0x15ff`) is free. Triggering Active Tag (`*My call:*` `360`-frame / 6-second cooldown) spawns the partner in slot `2` leaping in from `P1`'s screen edge with an entry strike while the outgoing fighter leaps back off-screen; once the entry strike completes (or immediately on landing/control recovery), `P1_STRUCT` switches to the incoming partner's fighter table and `StylePick`, resetting temporary move/charge state cleanly (`(user accepted)`).

  - **`1v2` Ambushes (`(user)`):** Slot `2` holds the second CPU enemy; while slot `2` is occupied, pressing Tag swaps the lead and partner **in-place on `P1_STRUCT`** (with a smoke/flash cue) without a slot-2 leap-in strike.
- **Tag Cancel (`*My call:*`):** Pressing the Tag input during a connected hit's cancel window triggers a **Tag Cancel**, spending half a Super/Drive stock (or starting the tag cooldown slightly longer) to extend combos across both fighters just like a crossover team game.

## Settled in grilling (2026-11-18)

Every item here is the maintainer's answer. Where this section and the text above differ, this section wins.

- **Words (`CONTEXT.md`):** Use **Match** / `in_match` (never "bout" / `in_bout`), **Tour course** (never "minigame" or "bonus stage"), **Tour run**, **Tour build** (never "loadout"), **Upgrade card**, **Loss burn**, **Grafted move**, **Specialist**, **Tag partner**, **Active Tag**, **Prop**, **Starting unlock**, **Lore school** (never "element"/"faction"), **Kit channel**, **Ailment** (never engine `status`), **Construct** (never "projectile"/"prop"), **Bootleg chip** (never "mutator"), **Cursed card**, **Omega mastery**, and the updated **World Tour profile**.
- **Drafts, Loss burns, Swaps, `4`-School cap, Starting Relics, Cursed cards, and `PURE RUN`:**
  - Drawn rounds (Double KO / tied Time Over) grant no draft; winning Round 1 opens `1` Upgrade card draft before Round 2, and winning the match-deciding round opens `2` back-to-back drafts at match end (final round pick + match-win bonus = `3` total).
  - Swapping a new **Lore school** onto an occupied **Kit channel**—or a new **Grafted move** onto a full `3`-slot fighter—transfers that slot's current Rank (`1–3`) and converts its banked cards 1-for-1 to the new school/move (freeing the displaced school from the `4`-school cap if `0` cards of it remain).
  - Loss burns enforce **leaf-first order** (`Rank 3 -> Rank 2 -> Rank 1`; **Omega masteries** `Rank 3 -> 1` before their host **Grafted move**; **Duo Capstones** and ***Eclipse* Keystones** before the last card of either prerequisite **Lore school**; partner-assigned Era Style and Grafted move cards before the Partner Contract itself; burning an Era Style reverts that fighter to their run-start style). An *Eclipse*-fused pair (*Satsui+Kyoi* or *Psycho+Soul*) counts as `1` combined school slot toward the `4`-school run cap.
  - **Cursed cards** are soulbound (unburnable in Loss burns) and clamp `max_sub_hp` across post-match heals, `ROUND_START` revivals, and Gill's `30%` Resurrection (floored at `1` Sub-HP) for **both Lead and any recruited Tag partner** across `1v1`, `1v2`, and `2v1`, while unrecruited scripted `2v1` AI allies keep normal `960` Sub-HP. All damage-over-time and cursed self-bleed/burn ticks floor at `1` Sub-HP.
  - An equipped **Starting Relic** counts as `1` burnable card in the tray (unless Cursed), acting as a Fight-1 safety net if lost early.
  - `PURE RUN: ON` grants zero mid-run cards and equips no Starting Relic, making it a **1CC Ironman** challenge (`1` match loss = Run Over) whose Country Boss clears still earn Starting unlocks.
- **Grafted moves:**
  - Only **direct-attack command normals and meterless attack specials** are eligible for Move Grafting; stateful installs (`Denjin Charge`, `Renkiko`, stance switches) are excluded from Move Grafting and represented as School/Major upgrade cards instead.
  - A Grafted move **overrides** any base move sharing that exact motion + button while equipped (labeled `REPLACES <MOVE>` on the card).
- **Constructs & *Soul Power* reflection:**
  - *Soul Power* guard-reflection and orbiting *Soul Orbs* reflect/intercept all traveling shots (native `0xffe100` projectiles plus launched *Soul Orbs* and hurled *Tengu Stones*, capped at `3` back-and-forth reflections per shot); grounded or body-tethered **Constructs** (*Fire Carpets*, planted *Psycho Mines*, unlaunched orbiting orbs/stones, and `12`-frame *Spirit Echoes*) are non-reflectable.
  - Active stage **Constructs** stay on the floor and orbiting **Constructs** retether to the incoming fighter across mid-round **Active Tag** swaps, all **Constructs** clear cleanly at `ROUND_START` and KO settle, and **Constructs** shatter Breakable **Props** in **Tour courses**.
- **Active Tag, Partner cap, and `2v1` Country Finales:**
  - A Tour run is capped at **1 Tag partner**; once recruited, subsequent partner cards in drafts become Partner Era-Shift / Rank-Up cards rather than replacing the partner.
  - Lead and Tag partner each keep their **own persistent Era Gauge state and their own 3 Grafted + Omega slots** across tags within a round (Grafted move and Omega mastery cards can be assigned to either fighter when drafted). Tagging out **pauses** active timed **Ailments** (Burn, Chill, etc.) on the benched fighter while the incoming fighter enters clean.
  - `2v1` Country Finales run on **`LifebarMode::Independent`** (`960` Sub-HP each unless lowered by a Cursed card). If `P1` is KO'd first while the Tag partner in Slot `2` survives, player control automatically transfers to the surviving partner to finish that round `1v1`. At the next `ROUND_START`, **both** fighters revive at full `max_sub_hp` in their original Lead (`P1_STRUCT`) and Tag partner (`Slot 2`) roles, and clearing Thailand always plays the **Lead fighter's** congratulatory ending (crediting both fighters on the Run Clear summary).
- **Tour courses:**
  - In side-scrolling Tour courses, `P1` faces the horizontal direction they walk (`Left` turns left, `Right` turns right), `CAMERA` ratchets forward one-way (*Super Mario Bros.* style, preventing backtracking past the left screen edge), and floor hazards / dodgeable Props inflict hitstun/knockback and `-TIME` penalties **without draining HP** (no `0`-HP KOs in Tour courses; `TIME` and score alone decide completion and Bronze/Silver/Gold drafts).
