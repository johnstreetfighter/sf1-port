# New overhaul art comes from the ROM's own sprites, not fan sprite sets

New poses for the overhaul are made only from the 1987 ROM's sprites: a fighter's own frames reused or pieced together, or generated with his own frames setting his look (another fighter's ROM frames may guide a pose) and held to his exact palette. Fan-made M.U.G.E.N sprite sets, other Capcom games' sprites and hand-traced references are not used, even as pose references, unless an artist gives permission. This replaces ADR-0003's plan to take pose keyframes from fan sets, because fan sets are other people's work built from mixed sources, and because starting from the fighter's own 1987 frames keeps his proportions and style. Fan-made move data (frame timings, hitboxes, move lists, descriptions of what a move does) may still be used as a reference.
