## Problem Statement

While the core **overhaul** unlocks all twelve **tournament fighters** from the 1987 **original game** and equips them with `SF1+` movesets and canonical later **era styles**, two major gaps remain for players and developers:

1. **Asymmetric Era Coverage Across the 1987 Cast:** Seven of the 1987 fighters returned in later entries, but the "Forgotten Five" (**Retsu**, **Geki**, **Joe**, **Mike**, and **Lee**) never received official 2D sprite sequels after 1987 (and several returning veterans missed entries such as *Street Fighter III* or *Street Fighter 6*). Restricting later **era styles** strictly to canonical appearances leaves five of the twelve **tournament fighters** locked out of `SF2`, `SFZ`, `SF3`, `SF4`, and `SF5` mechanics, gauges, and matchups.
2. **Missing 1987-Era Lore Challengers and Manual Asset Bottlenecks:** Over two decades, the M.U.G.E.N and Ikemen GO communities solved the missing 1987 boss animations (`SF1+`), engineered rich multi-era movesets for the Forgotten Five, and created 1987-era versions of thirteen pivotal lore characters tied to the first tournament (**Gou Hibiki**, **Gouken**, **Evil Ryu ('87)**, **Akuma**, **Goutetsu**, **Sheng Long**, **Cody Travers**, **Guy**, **Mike Haggar**, **Charlie Nash**, **Dan Hibiki**, **M. Bison**, and young **Chun-Li**). Transcribing frame timings, hitboxes, and pose keyframes by hand across twenty-five fighters and eight era families would be slow and error-prone, yet loading M.U.G.E.N `.sff`/`.cns` files at runtime would break 60 Hz rollback determinism, violate clean-room IP boundaries, and clash visually with the `384×224` 15-color 1987 arcade aesthetic.

Players want the option to experience these "what-if" **era styles** and 1987 **bonus challengers**—rendered in 100% authentic 1987 arcade pixel art and audio—purely as toggleable additions that never replace or alter the twelve canonical **tournament fighters** or their canonical **era styles**.

## Solution

Extend the **overhaul** with an offline M.U.G.E.N extraction and 1987 pose-synthesis pipeline (ADR-0003), optional **what-if styles** for all **tournament fighters** across every era they missed, and thirteen optional 1987-styled **bonus challengers**, controlled by explicit match and **ruleset preset** toggles (`enable_what_if_styles`, `enable_bonus_challengers`, and `enable_secret_arcade_intrusions`).

An offline extractor parses reference M.U.G.E.N character packages into deterministic fixed-point move tables, dual-depth and multi-box collision geometry, 4-to-6-button normal bridges, and aligned pose keyframes. Those keyframes condition the offline 1987 asset generator to produce authentic `384×224`, 15-color-plus-transparent `16×16` 4bpp sprite tiles and 8 kHz MSM5205 ADPCM + YM2151 FM audio packs. In-game, players can toggle **what-if styles** (exposing each era family's flagship revisions and loadouts) and the thirteen **bonus challengers** (complete with `[WHAT-IF] SF1`, `SF1+`, and later **era styles**, 1987-styled stage variants, YM2151 FM themes, full 5-country Arcade Mode campaigns with bespoke 1987 endings, non-punitive secret rival intrusions in Arcade Mode, preset lore episodes in 2v1 Dramatic Battle and 1987 World Tour, and online Room Browser filters) while keeping the twelve **tournament fighters** and canonical **era styles** one button press away.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Offline Extraction and 1987 Pose-Synthesis Boundary (ADR-0003):**
  - Never interpret M.U.G.E.N `.cns` or Ikemen GO `.zss` scripts at runtime, and never commit or distribute third-party `.sff` or `.snd` binaries.
  - Reference M.U.G.E.N archives reside strictly in a gitignored local developer directory. An offline extractor parses `.def`, `.sff` (v1 PCX and v2 PNG/LZ5), `.act` (768-byte palettes), `.air` (`[Begin Action]`, frame durations, `Clsn1` attack boxes, `Clsn2` hurtboxes), `.cns` (`[Velocity]`, `[Movement]`, `[Statedef]`, `HitDef`), and `.cmd` (`[Command]`, `[Statedef -1]`).
  - Floating-point velocities and gravity values are quantized offline into deterministic 16.16 fixed-point integers; damage, hitstun, and blockstun are normalized to the overhaul's 1,000 HP internal scale.
  - For 4-button (`X, Y, A, B`) M.U.G.E.N reference packages (such as Falchion22's 1987 conversions and Cody), the extractor maps Light and Heavy normals to `LP`/`HP` and `LK`/`HK` and populates Medium (`MP`/`MK`) frame data, hitboxes, and animations from the 1987 ROM tables (for **tournament fighters**) or 6-button reference packs (with interpolation fallback) so every fighter in `SF1+` and later styles has a full 6-button normal suite.
  - Extracted `.sff` frames aligned to their `(axis_x, axis_y)` ground-center pivot serve as pose-conditioning keyframes for the offline 1987 asset generator, which outputs `384×224`-proportioned frames quantized strictly to 15 opaque `xRGB_444` pens plus pen `15` (`0x0f`) transparent and sliced into `16×16` 4bpp tiles. All new voice callouts and SFX are newly synthesized/recorded and encoded into 8000 Hz 4-bit MSM5205 ADPCM and YM2151 FM formats.
- **Ruleset Toggles, UI Filtering, and Netplay Synchronization:**
  - Extend `MatchConfig` with `enable_what_if_styles: bool`, `enable_bonus_challengers: bool`, and `enable_secret_arcade_intrusions: bool`.
  - All three toggles default to `false` in `Faithful 1987`, `Classic Competitive`, and `Pure Footsies (1987+)`, and default to `true` in `Definitive Overhaul` (and are individually configurable in `Custom`).
  - Character Select provides a one-button filter toggling between `1987 Tournament 12` and `Expanded 25 Roster`, a style filter toggling between `Canonical Styles Only` and `All Styles (Incl. What-If)`, and visual `[WHAT-IF]` and `[BONUS '87]` pixel-art badges.
  - Online rollback netplay synchronizes `enable_what_if_styles` and `enable_bonus_challengers` in the host's `MatchConfig` at handshake and exposes both as visible room badges and search filters in the online Room Browser.
- **`SF1` Zero-Tile Boss Throw-Victim Mapping & `SF1+` Mike Normal Split:**
  - In raw `SF1` mode, map all ten CPU **tournament fighters**' existing 1987 knockdown and recoil tiles using per-frame `(dx, dy)` target-bind offsets and `flipx`/`flipy` flags derived from Falchion22's `Action 820` tables so every fighter can be thrown without adding new ROM tiles.
  - In `SF1+`, **Mike** uses standard kick animations on `LK, MK, HK` (including crouching kicks and jumping attacks); in his `SF2` through `SF6` **what-if styles**, **Mike** uses a 6-punch heavyweight boxer layout (Head Punches on `LP/MP/HP`, Body Hooks and Uppercuts on `LK/MK/HK`).
- **What-If Style Matrix and Flagship Revision Rule:**
  - When `enable_what_if_styles` is enabled, any **tournament fighter** or **bonus challenger** missing a canonical *Street Fighter* entry receives a `[WHAT-IF]` **era style** for that entry Family (`SF2`, `SFZ`, `SF3`, `SF4`, `SF5`, `SF6`).
  - Each `[WHAT-IF]` Era Family exposes that era's flagship revision(s)—`SF2 HF` (meterless) and `SF2 ST` (Super), `SFZ3`, `SF3 3S`, `USF4`, `SF5 CE`, and `SF6`—complete with full Loadouts (`A-Ism`/`X-Ism`/`V-Ism`, Super Arts I/II/III, Ultras I/II/Double, V-Skills/V-Triggers I/II) rather than fabricating synthetic intermediate patch revisions.
  - The Forgotten Five (**Retsu**, **Geki**, **Joe**, **Mike**, **Lee**) implement the moveset trees documented in the design specification (Shorinji Kempo kit for Retsu; Shuriken/Utsusemi/Claw kit for Geki; *Fist Combination* rekka, *Sobat*, and *Meteor Kick* kit for Joe; 6-punch motion slugger kit for Mike; Shadaloo C.R.I. branching *Open Canvas* rekka and *Upward Lance* kit for Lee).
- **The 13 Bonus Challengers, Campaigns, Stages/BGM, and Secret Intrusions:**
  - When `enable_bonus_challengers` is enabled, the roster adds **Gou Hibiki**, **Gouken**, **Evil Ryu ('87)**, **Akuma (Gouki)**, **Goutetsu**, **Sheng Long**, **Cody Travers**, **Guy**, **Mike Haggar**, **Charlie Nash**, **Dan Hibiki**, **M. Bison (Dictator)**, and **Chun-Li ('87)**.
  - Each **bonus challenger** includes a `[WHAT-IF] SF1` style modeled on Ryu and Ken's complete 1987 player state-machine rules (full crouch, jump, and block states governed by active **core toggles**), a balanced `SF1+` style, and their canonical and `[WHAT-IF]` `SF2`–`SF6` styles, plus a dedicated 1987-styled stage variant/remix and a 1987 YM2151 FM character theme arrangement (with Jukebox CD support).
  - All thirteen **bonus challengers** are playable as Player 1 through the 5-country Arcade Mode ladder and 1987 World Tour campaign with bespoke 1987-style pixel-art rival dialogues and character endings.
  - In Arcade Mode, when `enable_bonus_challengers` and `enable_secret_arcade_intrusions` are `true`, meeting performance milestones triggers secret rival intrusion stages without replacing the ten country opponents. Under the **Bonus Rival Exhibition Rule**, losing to an intruding **bonus challenger** costs no credit and offers an optional instant Rematch or clean progression to the next stage.
  - **2v1 Dramatic Battle** and **1987 World Tour** include preset 1987 lore episodes (*Ryu & Ken vs. Akuma/Gouken*, *Gouken & Retsu vs. Goutetsu*, *Cody & Guy vs. Mike Haggar*, *Gen & Chun-Li vs. M. Bison*, and *Sagat vs. Gou Hibiki & Dan*).

## Testing Decisions

- **Primary Seam — The Unified Frame-Stepped Scenario Session Seam:**
  - All **what-if styles**, **bonus challengers**, `[WHAT-IF] SF1` rules, `SF1` zero-tile boss throw-victim animations, Arcade Mode secret intrusions, 2v1 Dramatic Battle lore episodes, Style-Aware Utility AI behaviors, and rollback netplay `MatchConfig` synchronizations are tested through the existing top-level frame-stepped scenario session boundary (feeding a starting state + `MatchConfig` + frame-indexed player inputs, advancing frame by frame, and asserting external per-frame observables).
  - Tests assert only externally observable outcomes—per-frame fighter positions, active move IDs, health, **era gauge** levels, hit/block/throw outcomes, rendered `384×224` pixel buffers, sound-latch commands, training-overlay hitbox/frame-meter telemetry, and deterministic state-snapshot equality.
- **Auxiliary Seam — Offline M.U.G.E.N Extractor & Asset Pack Validator Seam:**
  - Automated integration tests feed synthetic/fixture `.def`, `.sff`, `.act`, `.air`, `.cns`, and `.cmd` inputs into the offline extractor and asset validator, asserting that:
    1. `Clsn1`/`Clsn2` boxes convert accurately into center-origin `(dx, dy, half_w, half_h)` `depth 2` / `depth 1` and multi-box collision tables.
    2. 4-button normal sets bridge into complete 6-button (`LP, MP, HP, LK, MK, HK`) move tables with deterministic 16.16 fixed-point velocities.
    3. Generated overhaul sprite packs strictly obey the 15-color-plus-transparent (`pen 15`) `xRGB_444` palette and `16×16` 4bpp tile constraints, and generated audio clips obey the 4-bit 8000 Hz MSM5205 ADPCM and YM2151 FM formats.
- **Prior Art in the Codebase:**
  - Follows the existing scenario runner tests in `sf-compare` and `sf-engine` and the differential toggle and snapshot round-trip test structure established in the core **overhaul** specification (`#91`).

## Out of Scope

- Embedding a runtime M.U.G.E.N `.cns` or Ikemen GO `.zss` script interpreter, or loading unquantized user `.sff` sprite archives at runtime.
- Committing copyrighted third-party M.U.G.E.N `.sff` or `.snd` binaries into the repository.
- Including joke or meme M.U.G.E.N edits (*Bootleg Ryu*, *Butthead Sagat*, *SHINGOMELTAKI*).
- Generating synthetic intermediate patch sub-revisions (`SF2 WW`, `SF3 NG`, `SF3 2I`, vanilla `SF4`) for `[WHAT-IF]` styles beyond each era family's flagship revisions.
