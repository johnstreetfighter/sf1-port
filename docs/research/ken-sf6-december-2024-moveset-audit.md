## Port comparison and reproduction

The native Ken Super Art dispatcher and training list share the Ken command table. The December motions are `214214K` for SA1 and `236236K` for SA2. The port had the two kick motions reversed. The native regression now checks every kick strength, Super cost, no SA1 fallback on double QCF with one bar, and no Super on double QCB with a punch. Both entries also exercise the Training Move List demo. Jinrai currently uses a single opener stream and a 35-tick state window, with low/mid/overhead chosen by direction on any fresh kick. Compare its four documented opener clocks and the distinct `6LK/6MK/6HK` branches before authoring new streams. Quick Dash currently uses a short port dash stream. Compare its source follow-ups independently from Drive Rush. To reproduce, check SHA-256 of the MMDK exports below. Index moves by `id`, use `fab.ActionFrame.MarginFrame`, filter proximity-only attack records, then resolve `AttackDataListIndex` to the zero-padded `HIT_DT` row and inspect `param["00"]`/ `param["04"]`. Use `ShotKey.ActionId` and the spawned action's `SteerKey` for projectile flight. Follow `TriggerKey.TriggerGroup` through `tgroups` to `triggers`, preserving `_Condition` and `_NotDefer`. Resolve catch and cinematic branches through `BranchKey` and `LockKey`. Live timing, Modern command priority and assisted combo timing remain open.
