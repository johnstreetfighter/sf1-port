# Ken moveset audit for Street Fighter V Season 1

The source is SFVSim's Ken game-script extraction `004`, labeled `1 [cpt-version]`. The executable, platform and exact Season 1 patch behind the extraction are unconfirmed. Capcom's January 2017 transition notes corroborate the preceding Season 1 Chin Buster, Inazuma Kick, Thunder Kick, V-Reversal and Knee Bash values. The Capcom move list supplies English names and depicts the later roster of system actions.
