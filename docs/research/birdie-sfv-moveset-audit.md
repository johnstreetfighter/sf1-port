# Birdie SFV moveset audit

Source comparison for [#149](https://github.com/johnstreetfighter/sf1-port/issues/149). The port offers Birdie in SF5, SF5 AE and SF5 CE. This audit uses both AE balances and separates CE launch and Seth from the later V-Shift balance. The port observations below are static checks against the working checkout at base `0e336743760689ccdd192cbe4ebce5c4d638c9c4`. Playable source platform, exact executables and paired input traces are open. ## Command inventory

Notation is facing-relative. `5` is neutral, `2` down, `4` back, `6` forward, `P` punch and `K` kick. These are advertised inputs, with motion leniency represented separately in the move pages.

| Family | Command and source branches |
|---|---|
| Normals and throws | Six standing, six crouching and six jumping normals. Bad Skull uses `LP+LK` neutral or forward. Bad Chain uses `4+LP+LK`. |
| Unique and target normals | Bull Drop `6HK`, Bull Charge `6HP`, Bull Slider `3HP`, Bad Hammer `2HP → HP`. Definitive adds a held standing HP route. |
| Bull Head | `236P` or `236PP` for EX. Each strength and EX has its own ordinary and VT-I action. Each VT-I version consumes 750 V-Timer. |
| Bull Horn | Hold any attack button 50 frames, then release. EX uses two held buttons and a two-button release, with one EX stock. Six ordinary button variants share action `910`. EX uses `911`. VT-I uses `986` or `987` for 750 V-Timer each. |
| Hanging Chain | `236K` or `236KK` for EX. K selects the trajectory. Holding delays release and a punch can abandon a held chain. AE adds VT-II variants using shared starts with different hit outcomes. Kage adds the middle-chain standing-MP cancel route. |
| Killing Head | `63214P` or `63214PP` for EX, at throw range. Script input rows encode forward/down/back with direction leniency. |
| Bull Revenger | `63214K` or `63214KK` for EX, with distinct approach strengths. VT-II variants first appear in AE. |
| Skip to My Chain | `236236P` uses 900 raw EX. Neutral and cancel script entries are `1200` and `1205`. Their contact window is `[73,78)` on the local script, before accounting for the speed command. |
| V-Skill I | `MP+MK` Break Time, `4+MP+MK` Banana Time, `2+MP+MK` Drink Time. VT-I neutral Break Time gets a separate timer-gain action. |
| V-Skill II | CE Chewing Time starts with `MP+MK` standing or `2+MP+MK` crouching. Hold the chord to enter the bubble state. Then LP pops it, MP and HP launch different trajectories, and K can enlarge it twice. |
| V-Trigger | Full gauge `HP+HK` installs Enjoy Time or Birdie Time according to loadout. During Birdie Time, `HP+HK` is Bull Swing and `2+HP+HK` is Bull Capture. |
| V-Reversal and V-Shift | Pepper Pot is `6PPP` during guard. The CE Winter source adds `HP+MK` V-Shift and another `HP+MK` after a successful evade for Bull Crush. |

## Late FAT cross-check

The March 2021 standalone export and February 2025 FAT export both give late 3/2 stocks, ordinary standing LP `5/2/16` startup/active/total, Bull Head L/M/H `14/3/45`, `18/3/50`, `27/3/53`, the 52/59/61 V-Skill I totals, and Bull Capture's down input. Both list 75 total for ordinary EX Bull Head, whereas the versioned source gives a boundary of 70 from v02.010. Keep both values identified until a paired run reconciles the elapsed and script clocks. The later FAT adds the charged HP row, and corrects ordinary Bull Charge from four to six active with recovery moving 18 to 16. Definitive action `679` and Capcom's March 2022 adjustment corroborate the ordinary change. Its powered FAT row still lists four active. The file dates identify exports. Individual rows can reflect different game builds.
