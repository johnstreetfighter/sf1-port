# M.U.G.E.N & Ikemen GO Community Research: *Street Fighter* (1987) Characters, Movesets, and Technical Integration Analysis

*Research compiled: October 2026*

## Executive Summary

Because Capcom's 1987 **original game** (*Street Fighter*) restricted human players to Ryu (and Player 2 Ken) while locking its 10 tournament opponents behind simplified CPU-only state machines, the M.U.G.E.N and Ikemen GO communities have spent over two decades solving the exact design problems facing `sf1-port`'s **overhaul** (`sf-overhaul`) and asset pipeline (`tools/asset-gen`):

1. **Completing the 1987 Roster (`SF1` and `SF1+` Era Styles):** Creators such as **Falchion22**, **HelloMyNameIsAAA**, and **Electro** extracted the 1987 arcade sprite sheets for all 12 fighters (**Ryu**, **Ken**, **Retsu**, **Geki**, **Joe**, **Mike**, **Lee**, **Gen**, **Birdie**, **Eagle**, **Adon**, and **Sagat**), synthesized missing player-state animations (crouching, jumping, blocking, throwing, and being-thrown frames that do not exist in the 1987 ROMs for CPU bosses), and designed complete normal, special, and Super Combo movesets.
2. **Demaking Later-Era & Lore Characters into 1987 Sprite Style:** Sprite artists demade canonically contemporaneous or sequel characters into authentic 1987 *Street Fighter* proportions and palettes, including **Cody Travers** (by **Falchion22**), **Charlie Nash** (by **Legorulez49**), **Dan Hibiki** (by **Jessica Smoke**), **Gouken** (by **Mugenmonkey**), and **Gou Hibiki** (by **Mugenmonkey** and **Pilgrim**).
3. **Reimagining the "Forgotten Five" in Later Era Styles (`SF2`, `SFZ`, `SF3`, `SF4`, `SF5`, `SF6`):** Because **Retsu**, **Geki**, **Joe**, **Mike**, and **Lee** never received official 2D sprite sequels after 1987, a collaborative M.U.G.E.N movement led by **David_Bazbar**, **Trololo**, **Zero de Armentis**, **R@CE AKIR@**, **Chuchoryu**, **Rabano**, **Duracelleur**, **0megaturb0**, **AnimugenZ**, and **RagingRowen** engineered complete multi-era movesets, command normals, rekkas, and custom sprite sheets for them—alongside retro 1987-costume adaptations of **Birdie** (by **Mazemerald / SFONE**) and **Gen** (by **mwryly & varo_hades**).
4. **Architectural Translation to `sf1-port`:** While M.U.G.E.N's runtime formats (`.sff`, `.air`, `.cns`, `.cmd`, `.snd`, `.act`) and Ikemen GO's `.zss`/Lua architecture cannot be executed directly inside `sf1-port` without violating our `16x16` 4bpp tile-pack hardware model (`sf-video`), 8 kHz MSM5205 ADPCM + YM2151 FM audio model (`sf-audio`), and deterministic 60 Hz integer state machines (`sf-engine` / `sf-overhaul`), their declarative data (frame timings, hitbox deltas, missing-pose sprite solutions, and moveset trees) can be systematically parsed and translated into `sf-overhaul` **era styles** and `tools/asset-gen` pose prompts.

### 1.3 How M.U.G.E.N Creators Solved Missing 1987 Boss Animations

In the 1987 **original game** (documented in [`docs/reference/`](../reference/) and [`crates/sf-engine/src/fighter.rs`](../../crates/sf-engine/src/fighter.rs)), only Ryu and Ken possess a complete player animation table (~45 distinct action states covering standing, walking, crouching, jumping, stand/crouch blocking, 3 punch strengths, 3 kick strengths, crouching attacks, air attacks, and knockdown/hit reactions). The 10 CPU opponents have severely asymmetric animation tables:

- **Sagat** has **no crouching frames**, **no jumping frames**, **no blocking frames**, and only a handful of normal strikes because his 1987 AI walks forward/backward and executes *Tiger Shot*, *Tiger Knee*, and *Tiger Uppercut* on ground level.
- **Mike** is a pure boxer who has **no kick animations**, **no crouching attacks**, and **no air attacks** in the 1987 ROM.
- **Geki** lacks a standard standing guard animation in several AI routines (relying instead on his teleport/jump evasion) and has no standard grab/throw sequence.
- **All 10 CPU opponents** lack the full set of M.U.G.E.N standard required states (`Action 5000–5300` in `common1.cns`: light/medium/heavy stand hitstun, crouch hitstun, air hitstun, ground bounce, lie-down, get-up, dizzy, and custom-throw victim rotation frames `820` at 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°).

Analysis of **Falchion22**'s, **HelloMyNameIsAAA**'s, and **Electro**'s `.sff` and `.air` structures reveals three distinct techniques used to fill these gaps:

1. **Torso/Limb Repositioning ("Cut-and-Shift" Edits):**
   - For **Sagat**, **Birdie**, and **Mike**'s missing **crouching idle (`Action 11`)**, **crouch guard (`Action 131`)**, and **crouch attacks (`Action 400–440`)**, Falchion22 sliced the upper torso and head from the standing idle/punch sprites, lowered them by `16–24` pixels over a bent-knee lower body constructed from knockback/walk frames, and repainted the waist seam using existing 15-color palette indices.
   - For **standing and air guard (`Action 130`, `Action 132`)**, creators reused the first startup frame of a high punch or recoil frame with the lead forearm shifted upward by `4–8` pixels in front of the chin.
2. **Frankenspriting / Head-Swapping onto Ryu/Ken Base Frames:**
   - Forcharacters with similar martial-arts gi or bare-chested proportions (**Retsu**, **Lee**, **Gen**, **Adon**, **Joe**, and **Geki**), missing crouching kick, jumping kick, and get-up frames were constructed by taking Ryu's 1987 crouching/jumping leg tiles, recoloring the trousers/skin to match the boss's 15-color hardware palette, and compositing the boss's head and torso on top.
3. **Axis-Offset and Rotation-Reuse for Throw Victim States (`Action 820`):**
   - Rather than redrawing 8-direction rotation sprites for every boss when thrown by Ryu/Ken's Judo Throw (`Ippon Seoi Nage`) or Back Roll Throw (`Tomoe Nage`), M.U.G.E.N authors mapped the existing 1987 horizontal knockdown frame (`fall` tile group) and vertical launch/recoil frame with `AngleDraw` or flipped `H`/`V` flags and per-frame `(x, y)` `BindToTarget` offsets.

> **Direct Lesson for `sf1-port` (`SF1` vs. `SF1+` Era Styles and `tools/asset-gen`):**
> In `sf1-port`, our `SF1` **era style** (1987 Unlocked) can make all 10 bosses immediately playable using **zero new sprites** by mapping crouching/air inputs to their closest existing 1987 hardware frames (exactly as **HelloMyNameIsAAA** does for strict arcade-accurate modes). For the `SF1+` **era style**, **Falchion22**'s and **Electro**'s hand-edited crouch, block, air-attack, and throw sprites provide an exact **frame-by-frame pose checklist** for `tools/asset-gen` so our AI sprite-delta generator produces clean, non-frankensprited 1987 tiles that strictly obey each character's 15-color `xRGB_444` hardware palette.

## Category 3: The "Forgotten" 1987 Roster Reimagined in Later Sprite Styles (`CPS1`, `CPS2`, `CPS3`, `CvS/POTS`, `KOF`)

When `sf1-port`'s **overhaul** transitions a fighter from the `SF1` / `SF1+` **era styles** into later **era styles** (`SF2`, `SFZ`, `SF3`, `SF4`, `SF5`, or `SF6`) or equips an optional 1987-pixel-art **era costume** (representing a fighter's appearance from a later *Street Fighter* entry while preserving 1987 pixel-art proportions and palette constraints), seven of the 1987 fighters (Ryu, Ken, Sagat, Adon, Birdie, Gen, Eagle) have official Capcom sequel appearances to draw from. However, the **"Forgotten Five"**—**Retsu**, **Geki**, **Joe**, **Mike**, and **Lee**—never appeared as playable 2D sprites in any subsequent Capcom fighting game (until Retsu's 3D World Tour NPC sub-boss appearance in *Street Fighter 6*). Furthermore, **Birdie**'s and **Gen**'s later Capcom appearances substantially altered their 1987 designs (Birdie changed from a tall white punk brawler in 1987 to a hulking chain wrestler in *Street Fighter Zero/Alpha*).

The M.U.G.E.N community solved both problems through a multi-year collaborative effort centered on the Mugen Fighters Guild threads [*SF1 Retsu, Geki, Joe, Mike & Lee CVS Beta* (`topic=139249.0`)](https://mugenguild.com/forum/index.php?topic=139249.0) and [*Joe, Mike, Lee, Retsu, Geki* (`topic=139549.0`)](https://mugenguild.com/forum/index.php?topic=139549.0), where pixel artist **David_Bazbar** released open-source custom sprite sheets that were subsequently coded and expanded by **Trololo**, **Zero de Armentis**, **R@CE AKIR@**, **Chuchoryu**, **Rabano**, **Duracelleur**, **0megaturb0**, **AnimugenZ**, and **RagingRowen**.

## Category 4: Technical Integration & Conversion Pipeline Analysis for `sf1-port`

To evaluate whether M.U.G.E.N and Ikemen GO characters can be imported directly or used as structured source data for `sf1-port`, we map Elecbyte's official M.U.G.E.N 1.0/1.1 specifications and Ikemen GO's architecture against `sf1-port`'s Two-Tier Hybrid Architecture (`sf-engine`, `sf-overhaul`, `sf-video`, `sf-audio`, and `tools/asset-gen`).

### 4.1 M.U.G.E.N & Ikemen GO File Format Specifications vs. `sf1-port` Subsystems

```
M.U.G.E.N / Ikemen GO Package                 sf1-port Target Subsystem
=============================                 =========================
char.def  (Manifest & Palette list)     --->  Roster / EraCostume metadata
char.sff  (v1 PCX / v2 PNG Sprites)     --->  tools/asset-gen -> sf-video 16x16 4bpp Tile Packs
char.act  (768-byte 256-color RGB)      --->  sf-video xRGB_444 16-color Palette Lut (pen 15 transp)
char.air  (Actions, Ticks, Clsn1/Clsn2) --->  sf-overhaul Animation & Dual-Depth Collision Tables
char.cns  (Constants & State Machines)  --->  sf-overhaul Deterministic 60 Hz Rust EraStyle
char.cmd  (Input Buffers & Statedef -1) --->  sf-engine::CoreToggles / sf-overhaul Motion Recognizer
char.zss  (Ikemen GO Zantei Script)     --->  sf-overhaul Structured Rust State Logic
char.snd  (RIFF WAV PCM Audio Container)--->  sf-audio 8 kHz MSM5205 ADPCM + YM2151 FM Patches
```

#### 1. Sprite & Palette Pipeline (`SFF` + `ACT` -> `sf-video` & `tools/asset-gen`)
- **M.U.G.E.N Specification ([Elecbyte SFF Docs](http://www.elecbyte.com/mugendocs-11b1/sff.html)):**
  - **SFF v1.01** (`ElecbyteSpr\0` header): Linked list of subfiles storing 8-bit 256-color RLE-compressed **PCX** images indexed by `(group_no, image_no)` with `(axis_x, axis_y)` pivot coordinates (where `(0,0)` is the character's ground foot-center) and external **768-byte `.act`** palette files (256 triplets of 8-bit `(R, G, B)` in reverse or forward order, with index `0` as the transparent color).
  - **SFF v2.00 / v2.01** (M.U.G.E.N 1.0 / 1.1): Binary header with separate sprite node table, embedded palette table, and `ldata`/`tdata` blocks supporting raw, RLE8, RLE5, **LZ5**, and **PNG** (indexed 8-bit or 24/32-bit RGBA) compression.
- **`sf1-port` Hardware Constraints (`crates/sf-video/src/{lib,decode,palette,render}.rs`):**
  - The 1987 **original game** renders a `384x224` visible region inside a `512x256` bitmap at `(64, 16)`.
  - Object sprites (`gfx3`) are composed of **`16x16` 4bpp planar tiles** (`32` bytes per tile across 2 ROM halves with bit-twiddled `invert(code)` addressing), where each tile selects one of 64 **16-color palettes** from `0xb00000..0xb007ff` stored as big-endian 16-bit **`xRGB_444`** (`4096` total RGB colors, **15 opaque pens + pen `15` (`0x0f`) reserved for transparency**).
- **Conversion & Integration Strategy:**
  - **1987-Sprite Conversions (Category 1 & Category 2):** Characters by **Falchion22**, **HelloMyNameIsAAA**, **Electro**, **Legorulez49**, **Jessica Smoke**, and **Mugenmonkey** already use 1987 `xRGB_444`-compatible color counts (<=15 opaque colors per character) because their sprites were ripped or edited from the 1987 arcade ROMs. An offline Rust tool in `tools/asset-gen` can parse `.sff` v1/v2 frames, remap color index `0` (M.U.G.E.N transparent) to pen `15` (`0x0f`), quantize each `(R, G, B)` triplet to 12-bit `xRGB_444` via `(r >> 4) << 8 | (g >> 4) << 4 | (b >> 4)`, slice the full-body sprite around `(axis_x, axis_y)` into `16x16` 4bpp tiles, deduplicate identical/horizontally-flipped tiles, and emit an extended sprite bank for `sf-video`'s Extended Scene Compositor.
  - **Later-Style Conversions (Category 3 CvS/CPS2/CPS3):** Because `sf1-port` maintains 1987 `384x224` visual consistency as a core design goal (avoiding mismatched CPS2/CPS3/CvS sprite rips at runtime), the high-frame-count sprites created by **David_Bazbar**, **Rabano**, **Trololo**, and **Mazemerald** should not be rendered raw in-game. Instead, their `.sff` animation sequences serve as **ControlNet / IP-Adapter pose-reference keyframes** inside `tools/asset-gen` to synthesize authentic 15-color 1987-pixel-art animations for later-era moves and **era costumes**.

#### 2. Animation & Collision Box Pipeline (`AIR` `Clsn1`/`Clsn2` -> `sf-engine` / `sf-overhaul`)
- **M.U.G.E.N Specification ([Elecbyte AIR Docs](http://www.elecbyte.com/mugendocs-11b1/air.html)):**
  - Plain-text `[Begin Action <id>]` blocks containing optional `Loopstart`, per-frame `Clsn1` (red **attack boxes**) and `Clsn2` (blue **hurtboxes**) or action-wide `Clsn1Default` / `Clsn2Default`, and frame elements:
    `group_no, image_no, offset_x, offset_y, duration_ticks, [flip], [blend]`
  - Coordinates `(left, top, right, bottom)` are relative to the character's ground-center `(0,0)`, with **negative `Y` pointing upward** (e.g., `Clsn2[0] = -18, -88, 18, 0`).
- **`sf1-port` Collision Model ([`docs/design/2026-10-27-definitive-overhaul-design.md`](../design/2026-10-27-definitive-overhaul-design.md) & [`crates/sf-engine/src/fighter.rs`](../../crates/sf-engine/src/fighter.rs)):**
  - The 1987 **original game** uses a deterministic two-tier bounding-box check (`0x43e82` in [`crates/sf-engine/src/fighter.rs`](../../crates/sf-engine/src/fighter.rs)):
    1. **`depth 2` (Outer Loose Box):** A center-origin rectangle `(dx, dy, half_w, half_h)` per animation frame.
    2. **`depth 1` (Inner Tight / Crit Box):** Checked alongside `depth 2` to determine clean/deep vs. partial hit damage.
- **Conversion & Integration Strategy:**
  - For **`SF1` and `SF1+` era styles**, a parser can convert `.air` `Clsn2` arrays into `sf-engine`'s `(dx, dy, half_w, half_h)` representation by computing the bounding union of `Clsn2[0..n]` for `depth 2` and the primary torso/limb box for `depth 1`, while converting `Clsn1[0]` directly into the active attack rectangle:
    $$\text{dx} = \frac{x_{\text{left}} + x_{\text{right}}}{2}, \quad \text{dy} = \frac{y_{\text{top}} + y_{\text{bottom}}}{2}, \quad \text{half\_w} = \frac{x_{\text{right}} - x_{\text{left}}}{2}, \quad \text{half\_h} = \frac{y_{\text{bottom}} - y_{\text{top}}}{2}$$
  - For **`SF2` through `SF6` era styles** in `sf-overhaul`, supporting multi-box hurtboxes (`head`, `torso`, `legs`, `extended_limb`) and multi-box hitboxes directly mirrors `.air`'s `Clsn2[0..2]` + `Clsn1[0..1]` layout, allowing 1:1 ingestion of POTS/CvS frame hitbox geometry.

#### 3. State Machines, Frame Data & Netplay Determinism (`CNS` / `CMD` / `ZSS` -> `sf-overhaul`)
- **M.U.G.E.N & Ikemen GO Specifications ([Elecbyte CNS Docs](http://www.elecbyte.com/mugendocs-11b1/cns.html), [Ikemen GO ZSS Wiki](https://github.com/ikemen-engine/Ikemen-GO/wiki/ZSS)):**
  - `.cns` defines `[Data]` (`life`, `attack`, `defence`, `airjuggle`), `[Size]`, `[Velocity]` (`walk.fwd`, `walk.back`, `run.fwd`, `jump.neu`, `jump.fwd`), `[Movement]` (`yaccel` gravity), and `[Statedef <id>]` blocks (`type = S/C/A/L`, `movetype = I/A/H`, `physics = S/C/A/N`, `anim`, `velset`, `ctrl`, `poweradd`, `juggle`) containing `[State]` controllers (`HitDef`, `ChangeState`, `VelSet`, `VelAdd`, `PosAdd`, `Helper`, `Projectile`, `Explod`, `TargetBind`, `PlaySnd`, `VarSet`) evaluated top-to-bottom every 60 Hz tick when `trigger1` / `triggerall` expressions hold.
  - `.cmd` defines `[Command]` motion strings (`~D, DF, F, x` with `time` and `buffer.time` windows) and `[Statedef -1]` transition rules.
  - Ikemen GO's `.zss` (*Zantei State Script*) replaces INI-style `.cns` blocks with structured `if / else / for` syntax and `map()` variables, while its rollback netcode serializes and restores the entire Go VM + character state snapshot on mispredicted remote inputs.
- **Why Runtime `.cns`/`.zss` Interpretation is an Anti-Pattern for `sf1-port`:**
  - Community M.U.G.E.N characters rely heavily on floating-point physics (`yaccel = 0.44`, fractional `velset`), arbitrary `var(0..59)`/`fvar(0..39)` state flags, unstandardized custom `HitOverride`/`TargetState` numbers (which cause character-splitting bugs when thrown, as documented in **Trololo**'s thread `topic=174671.0`), and `Explod`/`Helper` hacks for UI bars and input buffering (such as *Tiny Buffering* or *EXPLODsive Buffering*).
  - Embedding a M.U.G.E.N `.cns` interpreter would break `sf1-port`'s 16.16 fixed-point / integer 68000 physics determinism, bloat rollback state snapshots, and violate the unified **era gauge** and **ruleset preset** guarantees of `sf-overhaul`.
- **Recommended Static Extraction Pipeline:**
  - Instead of interpreting `.cns` at runtime, build a static extractor in `tools/` that reads `[Velocity]`, `[Movement]`, `[Begin Action]`, and `type = HitDef` blocks from reference M.U.G.E.N characters (**Falchion22** for `SF1+`; **Rabano**, **Trololo**, **Zero de Armentis**, and **R@CE AKIR@** for later **era styles**) to output strongly typed Rust frame-data tables:
    - Startup ticks (sum of `.air` frame durations before the first frame with `Clsn1`)
    - Active ticks (duration of frames with `Clsn1` while `HitDef` is active)
    - Recovery ticks (sum of remaining `.air` frame durations in the `Statedef`)
    - `HitDef` parameters (`damage`, `guardflag`, `pausetime` hitstop, `ground.slidetime`, `ground.hittime`, `guard.hittime`, `ground.velocity`, `air.velocity`)
    - Cancel routing rules from `[Statedef -1]` (`triggerall = stateno = ... && movecontact`).

#### 4. Audio Pipeline (`SND` -> `sf-audio`)
- **M.U.G.E.N Specification:** `.snd` (`ElecbyteSnd\0` header) is a flat archive of standard RIFF `.wav` PCM audio files (typically 11,025 Hz, 22,050 Hz, or 44,100 Hz 8/16-bit PCM) indexed by `(group_no, sample_no)`.
- **`sf1-port` Audio Model (`crates/sf-audio/src/lib.rs`):** `sf-audio` runs a cycle-accurate 60 Hz frame latch driving an **8000 Hz (`TICK_HZ`) MSM5205 4-bit ADPCM** voice/SFX decoder and a **YM2151 (`opm`) FM** synthesizer.
- **Integration Strategy:** Any new voice callouts for the "Forgotten Five" or later **era styles** (e.g., *Heavy D!*, *Gato*, or *Takuma* voice samples used as placeholders in M.U.G.E.N) **must not** be shipped directly from `.snd` files (both for copyright compliance and because raw 44.1 kHz WAV playback clashes with 1987 **audio fidelity**). Instead, `tools/asset-gen` should downsample newly recorded/synthesized voice lines to **8000 Hz**, encode them through an **MSM5205 4-bit ADPCM** encoder (`sf-audio::adpcm`), and play them through `sf-audio`'s existing ADPCM voice channel so every new special move sounds like it is coming directly from a 1987 Capcom arcade PCB.

### 4.2 Licensing, Attribution, and Clean-Room Boundaries

1. **M.U.G.E.N Community Norms vs. Open-Source Distribution:**
   - Within the M.U.G.E.N community, creators distinguish between **open-source releases** (explicitly permitted for modification and reuse with credit, such as **David_Bazbar**'s sprite sheets for Retsu, Geki, Joe, Mike, and Lee, confirmed by MFG administrator **JustNoPoint** and **Basara-kun** in [`topic=160896.0`](https://mugenguild.com/forum/index.php?topic=160896.0), and **P.O.T.S.** / **Basara-kun**'s open-source characters) and **closed/warehouse releases**. Even for open-source M.U.G.E.N assets, failing to credit the original spriter or coder (as seen in community disputes over uncredited sprite reuse in `topic=160896.0`) is a major breach of community ethics.
2. **Capcom / SNK Third-Party IP in M.U.G.E.N `.sff` and `.snd` Files:**
   - Nearly all M.U.G.E.N `.sff` and `.snd` files contain direct binary rips or derivative edits of copyrighted Capcom (1987 *Street Fighter*, *SFA*, *SFIII*, *CvS2*) and SNK (*KOF*, *Garou*) arcade ROMs.
   - Because `sf1-port` is a clean-room open-source **port** that requires the user to supply their own legally dumped 1987 `sf` MAME ROM set at runtime, **`sf1-port` must never commit third-party M.U.G.E.N `.sff` or `.snd` binaries into the git repository**.
3. **Two Clean-Room Ways `sf1-port` Can Leverage This Research:**
   - **Path A — Moveset, Frame-Data, and Pose-Delta Blueprints (Primary Recommendation):** Use the M.U.G.E.N characters documented in Categories 1–3 as **design specifications and pose references**. Implement the movesets (e.g., **Falchion22**'s 1987 boss movesets for `SF1+`; **Rabano**'s Lee rekka tree; **R@CE AKIR@** & **Trololo**'s Joe moveset; **Zero de Armentis**'s Geki; **David_Bazbar**'s Retsu and Mike) natively in `sf-overhaul` Rust state machines, and generate any delta tiles at build/ROM-import time by applying sprite-delta patches (or `tools/asset-gen` synthesis) directly against the user's local `gfx3` ROM tiles.
   - **Path B — Optional User-Supplied `.sff`/`.air` Asset Importer (`tools/mugen-import`):** Provide an optional CLI converter in `tools/` that allows an end user who has downloaded a M.U.G.E.N character pack (such as **Falchion22**'s *SF1* set or **Rabano**/**Trololo**'s CvS set) to locally convert `.sff` + `.air` + `.act` files into `sf-video` Extended Scene Compositor tile packs on their own machine, with full creator attribution displayed in the `sf1-port` UI.
