## Reproduce the extraction

1. Download the pinned MMDK JSONs above and verify SHA-256. Index `moves_dict` entries by `id` or `fab.ActionID`. Read `fab.ActionFrame.MarginFrame`, then follow `AttackCollisionKey` for strike, projectile or throw boxes. Omit metadata keys and proximity-only entries. `AttackDataListIndex` selects the zero-padded row in `HIT_DT`. Use `param["00"]` for ordinary standing hit and `param["04"]` for standing guard, and preserve raw fields and zero-based offsets.
2. Follow `ShotKey.ActionId` to projectile `pdata`, `SteerKey.FixValue`, collision keys and continuation branches. For normal actions follow `TriggerKey.TriggerGroup` to `tgroups`, then join its trigger row to `triggers`. `_NotDefer`, condition bits and the per-control input flags determine when an input may be buffered or dispatched. Keep action-local offsets when a branch jumps to a different action clock.
3. Follow `EventKey` unique-param entries for Denjin and `LockKey` damage entries for throws. To make a live recording from an installed copy, record the executable and version before exporting the Ryu tables with MMDK's dump facility. Use the source game to establish elapsed gameplay frames for cinematic actions and branches. [MMDK dump release](https://github.com/alphazolam/MMDK/commit/831564f1993926a9fff834108f24767748d40c06) · [PsychoDrive rectangle lookup](https://github.com/PsychoDrive-Dictator/psychodrive/blob/4bf6e9e7d67ebebaba126e3f1ad2c1c62449f27c/chara.cpp#L283)
